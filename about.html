Feature Suite

Built for Scholars, Creators & Thinkers

Everything you need to turn raw ideas, lectures, and articles into stunning handwritten documents.

🖋️

Organic Realism Engine

Uses a seeded mulberry32 pseudo-random generator to provide 100% deterministic layout across re-renders. Every glyph experiences minute rotation jitter, anisotropic scale transforms (scaleX, scaleY), and micro-shear transforms.

Deterministic PRNG Micro-Shear Ink Bleed
📜

Artisanal Paper & Layouts

Choose from authentic cream parchment, legal ruled paper, engineering graph grid, dotted bullet journal, and Cornell study layouts with dedicated margin markers and header boxes. Includes realistic procedural paper noise shaders.

Cornell Notes Parchment Shaders Multi-Page Sync
🤖

Generative AI Scribe

Direct integration with OpenRouter, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and local Ollama models. One-click workflows: offline Smart Arrange, Lecture Summarizer, Grammar Improver, voice-to-notes, and interactive study flashcards.

Multi-Provider Q&A Resequencer Study Mode
🔤

HandFonted Custom Font Studio

Create your very own handwriting font right inside the app! Draw alphabet glyphs on canvas or calibrate scanned worksheet templates. Features Ramer-Douglas-Peucker contour smoothing and instant in-browser OpenType TTF compilation.

RDP Smoothing OpenType.js IndexedDB Glyphs
📄

Lossless Multi-Format Exports

Export notes at crystal-clear 2× DPI for print or submission. Support for single/multi-page PDF documents with Compact/Standard/High presets, transparent PNG images, optimized JPG, scalable vector SVG, and one-click clipboard copying.

2× DPI Raster Vector SVG Size Presets
⚡

100% Offline-First PWA

Installed to your home screen or desktop as a native app. All application assets, fonts, and scripts are cached by the Service Worker. Notes automatically persist in browser IndexedDB, and real-time WebSocket collaboration is built in.

PWA Standalone Service Worker Collaboration
🛡️

Your Notes Are Strictly Yours

InkForge has no backend servers storing your personal writings or journal entries. The text layout, handwriting synthesis, font compilation, and PDF generation occur 100% locally on your own machine. If you configure AI keys, they remain locked in your browser's private storage.

Under the Hood

Engineered with Modern Web Standards

Built lean, clean, and fast with zero bloated frameworks.

⚡

Vanilla ES2022

Zero heavy framework overhead (React/Vue/Angular). A fully modular engine (23 focused scripts) with debounced renders and buttery 60 FPS transitions.

🎨

Canvas 2D Engine

Sub-pixel coordinate positioning, custom transform matrices (ctx.transform), anisotropic scaling, and procedural noise generation.

🧪

Test-Synced Releases

200+ Vitest unit tests and a Playwright E2E suite gate every release, with ESLint, Prettier, and version-drift checks enforced in CI.

🗄️

IndexedDB + LocalStorage

Dual storage hierarchy. Lightweight state keys stored in localStorage, while complete notebook trees and custom glyph curves persist in IndexedDB.

🔤

OpenType.js Compilation

In-browser TrueType Font (TTF) binary generator. Converts vector contours directly into standard font files ready for system-wide installation.

👥

WebSocket Collaboration

Optional real-time co-editing sessions over a tiny Node relay — shared cursors and live note sync without ever storing notes server-side.
